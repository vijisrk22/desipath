Overview The Desipath Community Rides module is a lightweight carpooling notice board for the NRI community. It allows Desipath users to post ride offers or ride requests, find other community members travelling the same route, and coordinate directly via in-app chat. There is no booking engine, no payment processing, no seat guarantee, no real-time GPS tracking, and no driver-for-hire functionality. This is fundamentally a community coordination tool — the digital equivalent of a notice board at an Indian temple or community center where someone pins a note saying &quot;Driving to the Garba event in Edison this Saturday, 2 seats free, WhatsApp me.&quot; Desipath makes this searchable, metro-targeted, and integrated with the existing NRI community. What this module IS: A structured, searchable notice board for community members to find and coordinate shared rides. Posts are informational only. All coordination happens between users directly via in-app chat or WhatsApp. What this module is NOT: A ride-hailing service, a taxi booking platform, a commercial driver directory, or anything resembling Uber/Lyft. No payments are facilitated through the platform. No driver background checks are performed or claimed as a safety guarantee. 2. In-App Chat — Coordination Flow Because there is no booking engine, all coordination happens through direct communication between poster and interested co-rider. Desipath provides an in-app chat thread per ride post. The poster can also optionally share their WhatsApp number for users who prefer that channel. 8.1 Chat Initiation Flow 1 User finds a ride User views a ride card in the feed that matches their route and date. 2 Connect button User clicks &quot;Connect.&quot; If poster provided a WhatsApp number: user sees a choice — &quot;Chat on Desipath&quot; or &quot;Chat on WhatsApp.&quot; If no WhatsApp: only &quot;Chat on Desipath&quot; is shown. 3 Chat thread opens Clicking &quot;Chat on Desipath&quot; opens a direct message thread between the user and the ride poster. The ride post is shown as a context card at the top of the chat thread. 4 Poster is notified Poster receives an in-app notification and email: &quot;Vivek Kumar is interested in your ride: Edison to Manhattan — weekday mornings.&quot; 5 Coordination happens User and poster coordinate departure time, meeting point, and any other details directly in chat. 6 No confirmation required There is no booking confirmation, no seat lock, no cancellation flow. If plans change, users communicate directly and the post stays active unless poster closes it. 7 Poster can close post When all seats are filled or the ride is no longer available, poster marks it as &quot;Filled&quot; or &quot;Expired&quot; in their dashboard. Post disappears from the active feed. 8.2 Chat Spec Spec Detail Thread type One-to-one DM thread between poster and each interested user. Not a group chat. Ride context card The ride post summary (title, from/to, date, seats) is pinned at the top of every chat thread for reference. Message types Text messages only in v1. Images in v2 (for sharing maps, screenshots of meeting points). Notification Push notification + email on new message. User can mute individual threads. Read receipts Single tick = delivered. Double tick = read. Standard messaging behaviour. WhatsApp handoff If user selects &quot;Chat on WhatsApp&quot; button, they are taken to a pre-filled wa.me link: wa.me/{poster_number}?text=Hi, I saw your Desipath Community Ride post for [ride title]. I am interested. Spam protection User can report + block. Three reports on a poster triggers admin review. Poster account with verified email only — reduces spam. Chat history Chat history retained for 90 days after ride expiry. Then archived (not deleted). 9. Legal Framework &amp; Disclaimer Requirements The legal safety of the Community Rides module rests entirely on one principle: Desipath is an information notice board, not a transportation service. This must be enforced consistently in language, design, and product decisions throughout the module. 13.1 Language Rules — Enforced Throughout the Module Never Use Always Use Instead Reason &quot;Fare&quot; or &quot;Price&quot; &quot;Fuel cost share&quot; or &quot;Sharing gas cost&quot; Fare implies a commercial transportation service. Cost sharing between community members is casual carpooling. &quot;Driver&quot; for commercial context &quot;Poster&quot; or &quot;Community member offering a ride&quot; Driver implies an employment or commercial service relationship. &quot;Passenger&quot; &quot;Co-rider&quot; or &quot;Community member&quot; Passenger is a commercial transportation term. &quot;Book a ride&quot; or &quot;Reserve a seat&quot; &quot;Connect with poster&quot; or &quot;Express interest&quot; Booking implies a reservation system. There is no booking. &quot;Guaranteed seat&quot; Never used anywhere There are no guarantees. This is a coordination board. &quot;Verified driver&quot; or &quot;Safe driver&quot; Never used anywhere. Desipath does NOT verify driving ability, insurance, or vehicle safety. Making safety claims creates liability. &quot;Ride service&quot; or &quot;Transportation service&quot; &quot;Community ride sharing&quot; or &quot;Carpooling notice board&quot; Service implies a commercial offering. 13.2 Required Disclaimers The following disclaimers are mandatory and non-dismissible. They must appear in the following locations: Location Disclaimer Text /rides hub page — footer Desipath Community Rides is a casual carpooling notice board for community members. Desipath does not provide transportation services, verify drivers, guarantee seats, or take responsibility for any rides arranged here. All coordination is between community members directly. Always exercise your own judgment about your safety. Post a Ride form — before submit By posting this ride, you confirm that this is a casual community carpool arrangement, not a commercial transportation service. Any fuel cost sharing is between you and your co-riders directly. Desipath is not responsible for any aspect of the ride. Connect button — before chat opens You are about to contact a community member about a shared ride. Desipath does not verify drivers, vehicles, or safety. Please use your own judgment. This is a community coordination tool, not a transportation booking service. Every ride detail page — header bar Community notice board only. No booking, no guarantees. Coordinate directly with the poster. Terms of Service — Rides section Full legal terms covering: no liability for rides, no commercial transportation license claimed or held, user responsibility for own safety, community members act as independent individuals not as Desipath agents. Legal Position: This module is legally equivalent to a community Facebook group or a temple bulletin board where someone posts a ride offer. No TNC (Transportation Network Company) licensing applies because Desipath does not arrange, facilitate payment for, or take any cut of transportation. The fuel cost sharing field uses language that is consistent with the IRS definition of casual carpooling (not a taxable income event when sharing actual fuel costs between co-riders). 14. Acceptance Criteria # Acceptance Criterion AC-01 A user can create a ride post (offer or request, any of the 3 ride types) in under 2 minutes on mobile. The form requires no more than 5 mandatory fields to complete a submittable post. AC-02 The word &quot;booking&quot;, &quot;fare&quot;, &quot;price&quot;, &quot;passenger&quot;, &quot;driver&quot; (in a commercial context), &quot;guaranteed&quot;, &quot;verified driver&quot;, or &quot;safe driver&quot; does not appear anywhere in the module UI, copy, notifications, or system-generated messages. Form validation blocks these words from appearing in user-generated post titles and notes.
